    Chiang Mai

    I wanted to take an overnight train from Bangkok to Chiang Mai, but I did not know that they are very popular and if you want a sleeper car, or even a seat that reclines all the way, you have to book it weeks in advance. So there’s your little tip if you want to do the same thing. I ended up taking a short plane ride there, so less adventure but much quicker, ha ha.

    Chiang Mai is in the northern part of Thailand and has been the center of Lanna culture for many hundreds of years. Before the kingdoms of Siam, and now Thailand, were created, Lanna culture operated independently from the Ayutthaya culture, which was centered near where Bangkok is today.

    There was a push to homogenize Thailand’s many ethnicities into a Bangkok-centric Thai “standard” during the 20th century, but thankfully there’s been a defense and resurgence of Lanna culture in recent years. And even beyond Lanna, northern Thailand is home to even more ethnicities like Karen, Hmong and Lao.

    I stayed in the old city, which is inside a square brick wall. Much of the wall is gone but remnants of it, and other ancient buildings inside, are attractions. Here are some street pictures of that area and others in Chiang Mai.

    Unlike the flat, river delta geography of Bangkok, Chiang Mai is located in a mountainous region. The highest point in Thailand is on Doi Inthanon, and I took a tour there one day. Along the way, we stopped at Karen and Hmong outposts to sample locally-grown coffee and tea and see various things they make. We hiked around the mountain a little bit, saw a very pretty rice growing area, and visited a couple of other spots. The hill tribes in this region used to grow poppies and make opium but switched to coffee a few decades ago.

    Unfortunately by the time we got to the highest point at Doi Inthanon, it was really raining and we couldn’t see anything, ha ha.

    Another (sunnier) day I rented a scooter and did the awesome Samoeng Loop ride that goes through mountains and forests around Chiang Mai. I hit a bunch of stops including Huay Tung Tao Lake (really a reservoir), Mae Sa Waterfall, Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden (which has a really cool forest canopy trail), the Samoeng Forest viewpoint, Ob Khan National Park and Wat Phra That Doi Kham.
