China May 28–June 3, 2026

Dali, Lijiang & Kunming

After Chongqing I did a few days in Yunnan province, hitting Dali and Lijiang, which have some wonderful natural scenery, and Kunming, which is the provincial capital and a very nice city. I think Yunnan in general is exceptionally beautiful.

Dali

Dali is the seat of the Dali Bai Autonomous Prefecture (home to the Bai ethnic minority) and is home to Erhai Lake, which will get us back on track with the wave theme:

I stayed in the Dali old town, which is a little bit out of the main town. It’s a pretty small place, but very fun to wander around, and tons of good food made by street vendors.

Lijiang

Another really beautiful spot in a mountainous area, really clean air and pretty scenery everywhere. I was also able to rent a scooter with some hostel buddies and that was very fun. There are also some pictures in here of Beiyue Temple, an important religious site for the Naxi ethnic group. I have been staying at a lot of hostels, which are cheaper and more fun than hotels. At this one, I played a fun game of Chinese Monopoly with a bunch of people. Instead of Boardwalk and Water Works and locations like that, the spaces were countries.

Kunming

As I mentioned, Kunming is the capital of Yunnan Province, and turned out to be one of my favorite cities I visited in China. It’s got about 8.5 million people but it doesn’t feel huge. Kunming was also a key strategic city in WWII, like Chongqing, and was actually the headquarters of the Flying Tigers.
