China June 3–10, 2026

Huangguoshu, Anshun, Guiyang & Guilin

With Sichuan and Yunnan provinces behind me, it was time to start moving east to the coast. Along the way I made a couple of stops in Guizhou and Guangxi Provinces, which are very mountainous and green, and have these really cool karst hills that pop up out of the flat landscape, the landscape is quite unique and beautiful.

Huangguoshu

My first stop after Kunming was the Huangguoshu waterfall, billed as the largest waterfall in China and part of the larger Huangguoshu scenic area, which features other waterfalls and is a central site for the Miao people.

I just spent a few hours here, but the park is large enough you could easily do a whole day. Here is a video slideshow because I couldn’t decide on one.

Guiyang

Guiyang is the capital of Guizhou Province, and I stayed there for three nights. I got out to see some neat parts of the city and tried a bunch of food, of course. One thing that people talked about there was the “Chinese White House,” which is actually called the Huaguoyuan International Cultural and Art Exchange Center, and there’s a hotel in it. The Chinese people I was with were surprised when I told them how small the White House in Washington, D.C. is. You can see it in this video:
