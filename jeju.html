South Korea August 14–21, 2026

Jeju Island

The final stop in Korea was Jeju Island, which is known for its natural beauty and as a popular getaway spot for Koreans. It has a nice, kind of wild feel, there’s one big city and then a bunch of much smaller ones around the island.

I spent most of my time in Seogwipo, which is on the south side of the island and is famous for having a lot of waterfalls and being close to excellent national parks that surround Hallasan, the volcano at the center of the island. Here’s the most spectacular of the three waterfalls I went to, called Jeongbang. It drops directly into the East China Sea.

One day I went to the Seogwipo Natural Recreation Forest, which was really neat. The day was very misty and rainy so a lot of people stayed away and I kind of had the place to myself. It was fun walking around the woods in that weather. It reminded me of the Pacific Northwest, except it was quite warm, lol.

I did a short trip to Beophwan Port where I took this video that has some of the volcanic rock that is all over Jeju in the foreground.

All over Jeju Island you will see these dol hareubang, or “stone grandfathers” that are carved out of volcanic rock.

After Seogwipo I went to Hyeopjae Beach, which is famous for its crystal clear water. It was really hot, so I went swimming in the morning, which was pretty fun. Nobody else was up that early. It was still like 85° F, lol.
