South Korea July 22–August 4, 2026

Sokcho, Wonju, Chungju & Andong

Sokcho

For my first stop after Seoul, I traveled about 2 and a half hours or so by bus to Sokcho. It’s a beach town that’s a popular weekend/short vacation spot for Seoulites. The location is really pretty, there is a mountainous national park surrounding the beach area which slopes right down to the beach, as you can see from this video.

And here’s the beach with some concrete jetty jacks, aka A jacks:

One of my favorite things about this visit was that one evening I was coming back to my hotel and I heard the front desk lady practicing accordion. She had the door and window to the booth shut, so I couldn’t see her, but the music was nice and audible in the lobby. I sat down on a couch and listened to her for a while, and recorded this little bit.

♫

And then of course there was the Seoraksan National Park, which was really great. It rained the first day I went, so I took this video of the Sinheungsa Temple, which dates to the 600s CE.

And then here are some photos of the park, including the Great Unification Buddha, which is a more recent addition, and a funny sign at a souvenir shop.

Wonju

Between Seoul and Busan, I was only staying three nights per city, so they were all pretty quick visits. Wonju was a 2ish-hour bus ride from Sokcho, and I chose to go there because I read that there is a really cool art museum and a kind of outdoor recreation area with a very long walkable suspension bridge in the mountains. Unfortunately I wasn’t able to go to either thing, lol because it was closed one day and the next day the bus I was supposed to get never came. C’est la vie, I still saw some other stuff.

The Wonju stream was right by my hotel so I could walk along it, as you can see from these couple of videos. You can really hear the cicadas in the first one, they were in full force during this period of the trip.
