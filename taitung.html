    Taiwan September 3–13, 2026

    Taitung, Hualien & Luodong

    I met back up with my friends on the east side of Taiwan, which is much more rugged, less developed and less populated than the west side. It’s where most of the aborigines were pushed over the centuries of settlement on the west coast, which has more fertile plains and space. A lot of the east side is mountains that slope down to the sea. So we rented scooters and spent quite a bit of time just driving up and down the coast, it was really fun and there was spectacular seaside scenery.

    Luodong

    Our final stop on the east coast was Luodong. We went whale watching, but we didn’t even see a dolphin, apparently the waves were too rough or something. I can vouch for that, I had never been seasick before but I can’t say that anymore. They almost canceled the boat because of the waves but I guess they figured it was OK. A lot of people were not feeling good on this excursion, lol. But it was all right, we all made it back to shore in one piece.

    The next day we went to a big seafood market a little north of Luodong. The idea is you pick out some seafood you want, then take it to a local restaurant that will cook it up for you.
