China June 10–16, 2026

Xiamen & Shantou

Xiamen

I considered getting back to the Pacific coast the start of my last leg in China, and Xiamen was my first destination. Xiamen is a big port city in Fujian Province, and was known as Tong’an County up until the early 20th century. It was one of four cities that in the 1980s were granted permission to create special economic zones that could experiment with market-based economic practices. It also has some nice beaches, too, although I must admit I didn’t actually swim at them. It rained much of the time I was there.

Shantou

Shantou is in Guangdong Province, and also was one of the four cities that were allowed to start special economic zones in the early 1980s (the other two being Shenzhen and Zhuhai). It’s also got some interesting architecture, specifically the Qilou style, which is kind of European-influenced but still Chinese. There’s a little part of town called, “Small Park” that is constructed this way. It also rained cats and dogs while I was there.
